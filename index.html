ABOUT US

Welcome to Liquid Wardrobe

Smelling good is a personal signature, but carrying a full bottle of luxury perfume isn't always practical. Liquid Wardrobe bridges the gap between premium scents and modern convenience bringing high-end designer and niche fragrances directly to modern nightlife, gaming, and wellness venues by the spray, while offering full-sized bottles online for fragrance enthusiasts everywhere.

OUR BRAND STORY

Crafted by Experience.
Driven by Innovation.

Liquid Wardrobe was born out of a simple observation: life moves fast, and your signature scent should keep up with you. Whether you're stepping out for a night on the town, hitting the gym, or trying your luck at the casino, looking and feeling your best should be complete with a touch of luxury.

Founded by serial entrepreneur, U.S. veteran, and Choctaw tribal member Steve McKinney, Liquid Wardrobe is built on values of integrity, service, and innovation. With decades of small-business leadership, Steve created Liquid Wardrobe to revolutionize how people access fine fragrance—combining cutting-edge automated retail technology with curated scent collections.

OUR MISSION

Mission Statement

To make luxury and niche fragrances effortlessly accessible, empower nightlife and hospitality venues with seamless revenue streams, and deliver exceptional scent experiences both on-the-go and at home.
